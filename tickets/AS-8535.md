  - Stale `requestPath` attribution — **CONFIRMED** in prod logs (`/ecs/prod-click2apply-app`): one pixel
    GET owning 666 lines over 9h14m across 222 applicants; 56,505 mis-attributed lines in 7 days. Cause is

**Blockers / Open questions**

- ⚠️ **The AS-8535 epic body describes a different system.** It specifies AWS Step Functions, fraud
  reduction as the driving goal, integration with a Business Rules Engine / Delivery Rules Engine /
  Event Hub, SNS ingress, and rollout by ATS partner and client tier. The design decided here is durable
  Lambda functions with per-board cutover. Decision: leave the epic body alone, carry context in child
  tasks. **Anyone reading the epic cold will build the wrong system.**
- ⚠️ **EAC readiness is the load-bearing assumption.** The design makes `easy-apply-connector` the sole
  delivery boundary, but AS-8535 lists ATS migration to EAC as out of scope and tracked under
  **CBS-2097, a prerequisite blocker**. CBS-2097 was ruled out of scope by decision. If EAC is not in
  front of every ATS, the per-ATS delivery fan-out has to come back.
- **`easyapplysite` collection contents unknown.** The MongoDB Atlas MCP is unauthenticated and no other
  connector reaches Mongo, so which boards are registered on `/v2/easyapply/:board` cannot be determined.
  This scopes the eventual v2 migration. Setting up a Mongo MCP was deferred.
- **Production feature-flag values unknown** — `.env*` and `config/local.js` are gitignored with no
  example file. Every "this flag is unset in prod" claim comes from an audit, not source, and they decide
  what is safe to delete.
- **`easy-apply-connector` is not an AS repo** (confirmed) but is the delivery boundary. Ownership for
  changes needed there is unresolved.
- **`apply-flow` has no row in the README repo inventory.** Deliberately left out of the `repos:`
  frontmatter rather than invented.
