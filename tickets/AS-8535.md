## 2026-09-16

**Did**

- Phase 1 grilling session for **apply-flow** — the new .NET 10 AWS durable-Lambda service replacing the
  Easy Apply job-board intake path. Ran to a shared understanding across 16 decision branches; design doc
  filed at [[wiki/AS-8535-apply-flow.md]].
- **Corrected the starting premise:** the 15 Easy Apply endpoints live in **`clickapply`**, not
  `hosted-apply`. Confirmed from source — `hosted-apply` contains zero board integrations, only one
  outbound POST back to `/easyapply/jobtarget` and one view reference. `easy-apply-connector` is ATS
  connectors, not boards.
- **Researched AWS Lambda durable functions for .NET** against AWS docs, the `aws-lambda-dotnet` repo and
  nuget. Confirmed GA, `us-east-1` availability, `dotnet10` runtime, the `DurableTestRunner` /
  `CloudDurableTestRunner` testing story, the four analyzer rules, and that **no compensation primitive
  exists** — a Saga must be hand-rolled, so the design uses forward recovery instead.
- **Audited the generic `/v2/easyapply/:board` path** for defects that would have been inherited by a
  base class modelled on it. Found **15 Class-A defects**, catalogued in the design doc. Outcome: the base
  class is now specified from the AJV contract rather than copied from v2.
- **Settled three claims** carried from the Easy Apply payload/branch atlases that source-reading could
  not reproduce:
  - `media_id` returns a **string** from `cloud-lookup-api` — **CONFIRMED**. `=== 29462` at
    `EasyApplyController.js:1687` can never match on the hashed-postingId branch.
  - Stale `requestPath` attribution — **CONFIRMED** in prod logs (`/ecs/prod-click2apply-app`): one pixel
    GET owning 666 lines over 9h14m across 222 applicants; 56,505 mis-attributed lines in 7 days. Cause is
    the single-slot tedious connection pool, **not** `AsyncLocalStorage` losing the store across `await`.
  - Oversize strip "frees zero bytes" — **REFUTED**. `ATSService.js:18-22` frees roughly a full copy of
    the résumé. The real no-op strip is `EmitterService.js:120-122`.
- Verified Terraform provider pins across the AS Lambda repos: **7 of 9 pin `~> 5.0`**, which cannot
  express `durable_config` (needs ≥ 6.25.0). apply-flow pins `~> 6.25`.

**Blockers / Open questions**

- ⚠️ **The AS-8535 epic body describes a different system.** It specifies AWS Step Functions, fraud
  reduction as the driving goal, integration with a Business Rules Engine / Delivery Rules Engine /
  Event Hub, SNS ingress, and rollout by ATS partner and client tier. The design decided here is durable
  Lambda functions with per-board cutover. Decision: leave the epic body alone, carry context in child
  tasks. **Anyone reading the epic cold will build the wrong system.**
- ⚠️ **EAC readiness is the load-bearing assumption.** The design makes `easy-apply-connector` the sole
  delivery boundary, but AS-8535 lists ATS migration to EAC as out of scope and tracked under
  **CBS-2097, a prerequisite blocker**. CBS-2097 was ruled out of scope by decision. If EAC is not in
  front of every ATS, the per-ATS delivery fan-out has to come back.
- **`easyapplysite` collection contents unknown.** The MongoDB Atlas MCP is unauthenticated and no other
  connector reaches Mongo, so which boards are registered on `/v2/easyapply/:board` cannot be determined.
  This scopes the eventual v2 migration. Setting up a Mongo MCP was deferred.
- **Production feature-flag values unknown** — `.env*` and `config/local.js` are gitignored with no
  example file. Every "this flag is unset in prod" claim comes from an audit, not source, and they decide
  what is safe to delete.
- **`easy-apply-connector` is not an AS repo** (confirmed) but is the delivery boundary. Ownership for
  changes needed there is unresolved.
- **`apply-flow` has no row in the README repo inventory.** Deliberately left out of the `repos:`
  frontmatter rather than invented.
- Remaining open questions are in the design doc under `OPEN QUESTION`.
