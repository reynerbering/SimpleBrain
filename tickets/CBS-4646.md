- **The headline numbers**, so they survive without opening the doc:
  - `value` is `varchar(500) NOT NULL` — D7 was right.
  - Only **113 sites** carry a `requires_pending_delete` row at all (108 = `'1'`, 5 = `'0'`), against **6,217** strict-`IsCloud` sites, of which only **12** are flagged.
  - Core V2 wrote **173,776** delete-status rows in 30 days across **754** sites. **99.3% (172,545)** are on sites with no flag — those are the close requests the decided rule would have suppressed.
  - **All nine of Shiela's sites, and SEEK site 25461, have no setting row at all.** So the rule cannot distinguish the reported sites from the rest — and for 25461 it would suppress the early-stop close request that PJO-11160 explicitly requires. **The decided rule would break the ticket CBS-4646 exists to unblock.**

- Tooling notes for whoever re-runs the queries: the MCP tool rejects CTEs and rejects `SUM(CASE WHEN EXISTS(...))` directly, and grouping `jobs_sites_status` by `created_by` over a 7–30 day window times out at 60s. Workarounds are recorded at the end of the doc's revision.
